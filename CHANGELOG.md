# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — Infinite Dominion

- Expanded the infantry line into six visually distinct upgrade stages ending with the Singularity Titan.
- Added unique map sprites and unit icons for all nine Aetherion units.
- Added the Quantum Fabricator and Worldshaper Architect builders with extreme movement and one-turn improvement speed.
- Added Genesis Garden and Continuum Spire, each producing 99,999 Food, Gold, Culture, Faith, Production and Science.
- Added the Continuum Ascendance religion and five original beliefs.
- Added six new economy buildings, culminating in the Omniversal Exchange with 99,999 of every major yield.
- Raised every city's passive output to exactly 99,999 Food, Gold, Culture and Faith per turn.
- Added new building, improvement, religion and belief icons and rebuilt the packed texture atlas.

## 0.2.0 — Absolute Dominion

- Added 10-Gold purchasing for all units and ordinary buildings.
- Added Faith purchasing for all buildings, including purchase paths normally unavailable for Gold.
- Added 999,900% Production toward buildings and wonders for reliable one-turn construction.
- Added +10,000% Strength, +50 Movement and +10 Sight to all military units.
- Multiplied improved luxury and strategic resource production by 9,999.
- Made the Quantum Core automatic in every Aetherion city and able to remove annexation unhappiness.
- Eliminated civilization-wide unhappiness from city count and population.
- Increased the civilization's starting economic yields and kill healing.

## 0.1.0 — Initial release

- Added the Aetherion Ascendancy, Archon Nyra Voss, three unique units, three unique buildings and Ascendant Physics.
- Added original artwork, texture atlas, documentation and JSON validation.
